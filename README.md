Vanilla is a macOS menu bar manager, forked from Ice by Jordan Baird. Hide items, bring them back when you need them, and arrange your menu bar the way you like. Vanilla Ice. You get it.

[Source](https://github.com/kimobu/vanilla) · [Releases](https://github.com/kimobu/vanilla/releases) · [Report a bug](https://github.com/kimobu/vanilla/issues) · [GPL-3.0 license](LICENSE)

## Features

- Hide menu bar items, with a separate always-hidden section.
- Show hidden items in the Vanilla Bar below the menu bar.
- Arrange items by dragging them in settings, or find them with search.
- Show items on hover, click, scroll, or a keyboard shortcut, then automatically hide them again.
- Customize the menu bar's tint, shadow, border, and shape.
- Adjust menu bar item spacing and launch at login.

## Requirements

macOS 26 or 27.

## Getting started

Launch Vanilla and follow its permission setup for Accessibility and Screen Recording. Open Settings → Menu Bar Layout to drag items between Visible, Hidden, and Always Hidden. Enable the Always Hidden section in Advanced settings if you need it.

Click the Vanilla menu bar icon to access hidden items. General settings control the Vanilla Bar, automatic hiding, and launch at login. Hotkeys settings let you assign shortcuts for search and section controls.

On first launch, Vanilla imports your Ice settings without changing Ice. Existing Vanilla settings take precedence.

## Updates and feedback
